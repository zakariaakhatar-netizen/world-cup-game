How to play

1. Teams take turns to pick a ball.
2. Answer the question. ⚽ Goal ball: +10. 🏆 Trophy ball: +20. Wrong answer: 0.
3. 🟥 Red card ball: no question, the team loses 5 points.
4. The teacher can start / stop the timer and change any score at any time.
5. When all balls are used, the highest score wins.
